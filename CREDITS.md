# They Were Here — Credits

## What is They Were Here?

They Were Here is a location-locked note treasure hunt PWA built for the LAYOVER 2026 hackathon by TinkerHub at TinkerSpace Calicut.

## Team

- **Alfie** — Architecture, map engine, reveal system, state management, deployment
- **Tippu** — UI components, feature screens, utilities, documentation, testing

## AI Tools Disclosure

This product contains **no AI features** and makes **no AI claims**. It is deterministic and secure by design.

AI tools were used during development:
- **Google Antigravity** — AI pair programming assistant
- **Google Stitch** — Design reference generation
